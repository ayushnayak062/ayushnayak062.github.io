Multiplayer Co-op VR Zombie Shooter

Project Summary

Contributed to the development of a multiplayer co-op VR shooter focused on free-roam, wave-based zombie combat. The project emphasized immersive gameplay, enemy AI behavior, and physical feedback integration for location-based VR experiences.

My Role & Responsibilities

  • Implemented core gameplay loops for a co-op VR shooter, including combat flow and progression logic
  • Designed and implemented enemy AI systems for navigation, combat states, and difficulty scaling
  • Integrated third-party haptic suit APIs to enhance physical feedback during gameplay
  • Built an automated headset–haptic suit pairing workflow to streamline session setup
  • Collaborated with designers and testers to refine gameplay pacing and player feedback
  • Assisted with build testing, bug reproduction, and gameplay balancing across multiple VR setups

Technical Focus / Systems Worked On

  • Unity (C#) gameplay and VR systems
  • Multiplayer co-op gameplay logic (session-based)
  • Enemy AI behavior systems:
    • Navigation
    • Combat states
    • Difficulty scaling
  • Haptic feedback integration using external hardware APIs
  • Automated device allocation and pairing systems
  • Iterative gameplay tuning through internal playtesting
  • Cross-hardware VR testing and stability validation

Constraints

  • Developed as part of a professional role under NDA
  • No public builds, gameplay footage, or source code
  • Hardware-dependent setup (VR headsets and haptic suits)
  • Performance and stability requirements for location-based VR environments
  • Iterative development with frequent testing and balance adjustments

Outcomes & Learnings

  • Gained hands-on experience building multiplayer VR gameplay systems
  • Learned to design AI behaviors that remain readable and fair in VR environments
  • Improved understanding of hardware integration and session management workflows
  • Strengthened collaboration skills across gameplay, UX, and QA disciplines
